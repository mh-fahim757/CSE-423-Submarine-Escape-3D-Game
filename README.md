Submarine Escape 3D Game
--------------------------
Features:
---------
1) Submarine Collision – Colliding with obstacles reduces submarine health.
2) Speed Burst (Cheat Mode) – Press C for a temporary speed boost.
3) Game Over Screen – Displays “GAME OVER” when health reaches zero or too many shots are missed.
4) Obstacle Respawn – Destroyed or moved obstacles reappear at random locations.
5) Scoring System – Points increase when torpedoes successfully hit obstacles.
6) Dynamic Difficulty Scaling – Over time, the game introduces more/faster obstacles.
7) Third-Person Camera – Smooth camera follows the submarine from behind.
8) Health System – Submarine starts with fixed HP; reduced by collisions or enemy attacks.
9) Random Obstacles & Movement – Obstacles spawn randomly; some drift/move unpredictably.
10) Torpedo Collisions – Torpedoes destroy obstacles upon impact.
11) Arena Limits – Torpedoes disappear after leaving the play area.
    
Abilities & Power-Ups
---------------------
12) Stealth Mode (V Key) – Submarine becomes semi-invisible. Obstacles cannot detect it, but speed is halved.
13) Power-Ups – Random item drops:
14) Health Pack (+20 HP)
15) Torpedo Boost (rapid fire for a short duration)
16) Shield (absorbs one hit)
17) Sonar Ping – Sends out a sonar wave to temporarily reveal hidden obstacles, treasures, or enemies.

Environment & Immersion
------------------------
18) Bubble Trail Effect – Submarine leaves rising bubbles behind, adding underwater atmosphere.
19) Dynamic Lighting & Darkness Zones – Certain regions are dark; submarine has a limited light cone. Power-ups can extend visibility.
20) Treasure Chests & Collectibles – Hidden crates in caves contain points, upgrades, or story clues.
21) Depth Pressure Challenge – Increasing water pressure at deeper levels slowly damages the submarine unless reinforced.
22) Underwater Currents (Wind Zones) – Specific areas apply directional forces, pushing or pulling the submarine.

Enemies & Challenges
---------------------
24) Boss Obstacle (Every 5th Minute) – A giant sea monster with high health appears. Requires multiple hits and rewards major points when defeated.
25) Minimap Radar (HUD) – Displays nearby obstacles and torpedoes using a 2D orthographic top right minimap.
